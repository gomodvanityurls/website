How to upgrade#

Anonymous → Verified (free)#

gvu auth bind user@example.com
# Enter the 6-digit code sent to your email

That’s it. You now have 10 routes and 1 custom domain.

Verified → Pro (coming soon)#

Subscription-based. 50 routes + 50 custom domains.

Notes#

  • All route types share a single quota pool
  • For example, a Verified user with 1 custom domain route has 9 remaining slots for gomod.io routes
  • gomod.io is the platform’s default domain — works immediately without DNS configuration
  • Custom domains require a DNS CNAME record pointing to the service
